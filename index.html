How it works

Three entries, one clearer picture.

Why we built this as a free first step

Most people never see a financial plan until they're sold a product first. We wanted a way for anyone to check their own numbers, honestly, before spending a rupee on advice — and to make the idea of financial planning feel approachable rather than intimidating.

If your snapshot shows gaps worth addressing, that's exactly what a full consultation is for — a plan built on your complete picture, not five quick answers.
